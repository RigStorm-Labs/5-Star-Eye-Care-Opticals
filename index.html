Premium Eye Care
Services

Advanced optometric services delivered by experienced professionals with state-of-the-art equipment.

👁️

Comprehensive Eye Examination

Full eye health assessment covering visual acuity, intraocular pressure, retinal health, and more. Essential for early detection of glaucoma, cataracts, and macular degeneration.

  • Visual acuity & refraction test
  • Slit lamp biomicroscopy
  • Tonometry (eye pressure)
  • Fundus examination
  • Color vision assessment
From ₹500
👓

Prescription Eyewear

Hundreds of frames paired with precision-cut lenses made to your exact prescription. From single vision to progressive multifocal lenses.

  • Single vision lenses
  • Bifocal lenses
  • Progressive/multifocal
  • Anti-reflective coatings
  • Blue light filtering
  • Photochromic (Transitions)
From ₹1,200
🔵

Contact Lens Fitting

Expert contact lens consultation and fitting for daily, monthly, and specialized lenses. We carry all major brands at competitive prices.

  • Initial fitting consultation
  • Trial lens fitting
  • Toric lenses for astigmatism
  • Multifocal contact lenses
  • Monthly supply packages
From ₹300/month
👶

Pediatric Eye Care

Specialized vision examinations for children from age 3 onwards. Early detection of lazy eye, squint, and developmental vision problems.

  • Age-appropriate vision tests
  • Amblyopia (lazy eye) detection
  • Squint assessment
  • Child-friendly frames selection
  • Myopia management programs
From ₹400
🌟

Premium Lens Coatings

Enhance the performance and longevity of your lenses with our range of advanced optical coatings tailored to your lifestyle.

  • Anti-reflective (AR) coating
  • Blue light protection
  • Scratch-resistant hardcoat
  • UV 400 protection
  • Hydrophobic & oleophobic
From ₹350

Low Vision Rehabilitation

Specialized support for patients with vision loss from conditions like macular degeneration, diabetic retinopathy, and glaucoma.

  • Low vision assessment
  • Magnification device fitting
  • Adaptive strategies guidance
  • Specialist referral network
  • Follow-up care program
Consultation ₹600
